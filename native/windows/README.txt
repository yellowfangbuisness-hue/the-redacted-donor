THE REDACTED DONOR - Windows version
A detective story about organ trafficking. No gore.

HOW TO PLAY
  1. Extract the whole folder from the zip (right-click the zip, Extract All).
  2. Double-click "Play The Redacted Donor.cmd".
     The game opens in its own window. Press F11 for full screen.

  You can also open index.html directly in Edge, Chrome or Firefox.
  Keep the files in this folder together.

WHAT YOU NEED
  - Windows 10 or 11.
  - Microsoft Edge (already on Windows) or Google Chrome.
  - A graphics card or laptop chip from roughly the last eight years (WebGL 2).
  Nothing is installed and the game runs without the internet.

IF WINDOWS SHOWS A WARNING
  Files that came from the internet are flagged. If you see "Windows protected your PC",
  choose More info, then Run anyway. To avoid it, right-click the zip before extracting,
  choose Properties, tick Unblock, then extract.
  The .cmd file only starts your browser with the game file; open it in Notepad to read it.

CONTROLS
  W A S D walk, mouse looks, E interacts, C crouches, Shift runs, G throws,
  B opens the kit, J the journal, Esc pauses. A controller also works.
  The full list is under Controls on the title screen.

YOUR PROGRESS
  Saved by the browser the game opened in, on this PC. If you move the folder,
  or switch between Edge and Chrome, the save does not follow. Use the transfer
  code in Pause (cloud save) to carry it across.

HAND TRACKING (optional, works without the internet)
  Turn on "Spatial Hand Tracking" on the title screen. The browser asks for the camera
  each time. Everything it needs is in hand-tracking.js in this folder, so keep that
  file beside index.html. The camera picture never leaves your PC.
  - Close your hand to grab, open it to let go. Move it toward a screen edge to look.
  - Turn a dial or a lock wheel by sliding your closed hand sideways, or by turning
    your wrist.
  - H turns hand tracking on or off at any time. Pause has the camera choice, look
    speed, "Recalibrate hands" and the hand engine (smooth or classic).
  - The line under the hand panel shows how it is running, for example
    "30 a second, 8 ms, background". If it says "Too dark", add light.

NEEDS THE INTERNET
  - Leaderboards, accounts and cloud saves.

BEFORE A PRESENTATION (gaming laptops with two graphics chips)
  1. Plug the laptop in and pick its performance mode.
  2. Make the browser use the fast graphics card: Windows Settings > System > Display >
     Graphics, add Microsoft Edge (or Chrome), Options, High performance. Restart it.
  3. Run the launcher, press F11, turn hand tracking on and go through the hand setup
     once on this laptop, in the room's own lighting, sitting where you will present.
     Light your hands from the front; a bright window behind you makes them hard to see.
  4. Keep one hand in view unless you are turning a page with two.
  5. If hand tracking misbehaves on the day: Pause > Hand engine: classic, or press H
     and carry on with the mouse. Nothing is lost.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
  - A blank or black window: update your graphics driver, or try Chrome.
  - Slow on an older laptop: the game lowers its own resolution after a few seconds.
    Veteran Investigator Mode on the title screen is also gentler.
  - The window is too big or small: resize it, or press F11.
